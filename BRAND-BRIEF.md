# VIOLETA — Master Brand & Website Brief

## Positioning
VIOLETA is an independent interior design, custom furniture and execution brand in Saudi Arabia. Do not describe it as a registered company unless its legal status changes.

## Core promise
**Arabic:** عندك مساحة؟ خلّنا نصمّمها لك ونوريك وش ممكن تصير.
**English:** Got a space? Let us design it and show you what it can become.

## Services
- تصميم داخلي 3D
- مخططات تنفيذية
- تنفيذ الديكورات والأثاث
- تنفيذ مخصص حسب الطلب
- تشطيبات
- تأثيث واختيار الخامات والإكسسوارات حسب نطاق المشروع

## Tone
Saudi conversational Arabic: simple, polished, warm and direct. Avoid exaggerated luxury clichés and generic slogans. English should sound natural rather than literal translation.

## Visual identity
- Deep Teal: #0B303B
- Warm Gold: #C5A16B
- Warm Ivory: #F7F3ED
- Sand/Taupe: #E8DDCE
- Dark Ink: #172226
- Arabic: Tajawal
- English: Manrope
- Style: Contemporary + Architectural + Warm Premium
- Use the VIOLETA V / arch geometry as a restrained brand motif.
- Large project imagery, generous whitespace, calm motion and clean typography.

## Customer journey
Content / social → Website → Start your project → Project type → Service → Location → Scope → Area → Budget → Contact → WhatsApp → Consultation → Design / quotation → Execution.

## How we work
1. Start your project / send initial brief.
2. Discuss the idea, needs, preferred direction and budget.
3. Develop concept, 3D design, materials and technical drawings according to scope.
4. Confirm measurements, scope, timeline and execute through handover.

## Why VIOLETA
- Engineering-level design and execution experience
- Clear plan tailored to each space and budget
- Project supervision through handover
- Clear studied timeline
- Quality and clear material/execution specifications
